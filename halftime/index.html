How to read it

Fair: expected finish with a normal-rate 2nd half. HOT plays finish above it about half the time.

Over ≤: bet the Over only if the live line is at or below this. HOT = Fair − 1 (Over hit ~68% singles / 63% combos); COLD = Fair − 2 (~63%).

Live: Pick6's live line (one number = both sides; blank = not posted, check the app). Pick6 posts no live Rebounds or PRA, and combos are More-only.

Bet: study hit rate for the better side at that line, halftime only. ✅ at 60%+, highlighted green.

Players are sorted by 1H minutes ÷ projected minutes: 55–60% (best), 50–55%, 60%+, under 50%. 3+ fouls sort last.
